This directory contains latin-subset, variable-weight WOFF2 builds of:

  - Space Grotesk   (space-grotesk-latin.woff2)
    Copyright (c) Florian Karsten.
    https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk

  - JetBrains Mono  (jetbrains-mono-latin.woff2)
    Copyright (c) JetBrains s.r.o.
    https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
Full license text: https://openfontlicense.org

They are bundled (self-hosted, no CDN) so the configurator renders identically
on Linux — the primary platform for this tool — and so all assets stay inside
the page with no third-party requests, matching the tool's privacy promise.
