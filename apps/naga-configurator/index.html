What works now The 2-button, 6-button and 12-button side plates are validated for guarded readback and normal on-board writes. This project is independent and not affiliated with Razer.
Browser support Use Chromium, Chrome or Edge over HTTPS. On Linux, close VM USB passthrough and make sure udev/hidraw permissions allow browser access to the Razer HID interface.
Default presets Keyboard, mouse, disable, double-click, media, DPI/profile cycle, and scroll-mode toggle are available. Hypershift comes later.
